### Design review (v1)

Question: does the proposal solve the user's problem?

- Challenge assumptions and the alternatives considered; check authority boundaries, failure
  modes, migration and compatibility.
- Inspect the repository contracts the proposal relies on rather than trusting the document.
- Required design decisions must be resolved before implementation; list each unresolved one.
- Judge the design as a proposal. Do not require implementation evidence, and do not treat
  approval as implementation approval.
