## Confounders, failures and repairs

- **Same-task reuse:** native T-244 explicitly ported the reviewed first arm;
  both review trees were 46afb1fef02e64754c8c3e6cb100265221d1687f.
  Its savings compare implementation with reuse. Shared retained branches
  and board summaries also exposed later workers to solutions. Later
  independence instructions did not make the study blinded.
- **Implementation breadth:** T-249 permitted alternatives; native also filtered
  terminal members of a live grant. Identical specs did not force equal breadth.
- **Owner wait:** T-245 native's single Needs-me interval includes 17,275.993
  seconds awaiting an owner-escalated method choice. PM answering was refused;
  only the actual owner answer resumed the same generation. Wall totals are
  not pure implementation latency.
- **Detection surfaces:** OmO uses idle_no_report warning events; native uses
  canonical no_report/no_verdict turn classes. These are unequal instruments.
  T-245's unregistered OmO prerequisite hold required a controller clarification;
  an early absence of warnings did not prove no hold. Two later warnings count.
- **Rescues:** counts are canonical audit events, not unique interventions.
  T-246 OmO's one effective pane delivery produced two entries (manual observation
  plus structured audit); T-245 adds one prerequisite rescue. T-180 native's
  oversized undelivered repair instruction was cancelled and replaced by a
  concise supported reply, producing two actions. The audit is not erased and
  these are not claimed as five distinct rescues.
- **T-246 quality and gate burden:** native review 1 reproduced an exhausted
  text-budget score mismatch (16,016 incremental versus 8,016 canonical),
  repaired in the same generation. Its raw first turn separately records
  provider overload; zero silent-ending classes does not erase that error.
  OmO passed initial quick review but failed the full work-budget stage.
  Equivalent frozen-base counters reproduced retained whole-source work;
  the repair removed the fallback without weakening counters or budgets.
  An intermediate full gate caught a journal-unaware integration fixture.
  All failures, repair usage and later full PASS count. OmO's window therefore
  includes full gates; the final native T-180 window does too. Other windows
  are quick: rates are not equal workloads despite the unchanged contract.
- **T-180 quality:** OmO review 1 caught foreign/unknown session-ownership
  probe/prompt behavior. Native review 1 caught aggregate acceptance reviving
  cancellation, cancelled targets blocking global reconciliation, and refused
  owner-origin cancellation publishing its terminal marker. Both were repaired
  in the original runs. OmO was approved on review 2. Native was reopened
  after review 2 by a full-gate task-list work-budget failure (3 scans versus
  a budget of 2); it repaired the extra scan, passed the full gate with 3,581
  tests, and was independently approved on the third and final review.
- **Delivery:** T-246's stored OmO repair and supported resend reported
  session_unbound despite an open original session. Exact text was delivered
  once there, acknowledged, marked observed and audited. No new session or
  worker was substituted. Other repairs used canonical inbox delivery.
  Temporary review capacity refusals allocated no review or policy exception.

The final third review approved at 04:12:37Z, but the expected observer

wall-to-approval metric and must not be mistaken for model execution time.
